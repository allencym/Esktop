Privacy Policy

Esktop is a free web desktop and app store. We built it to be simple and private. This policy explains what we collect — and what we don't collect — when you use esktop.com.

1. The Short Version

In V1, we don't have accounts. You can try the demo without signing up. Your notes and to-do items are saved only in your own browser using LocalStorage. We don't see them, we don't sync them, and we can't read them.

When you click Get in the App Store, you leave esktop.com and go to the official publisher's website. That publisher's privacy policy applies there, not ours.

2. What We Collect

We do not collect:

  • Your notes, files, or to-do items in the demo
  • Your name, email, or password in V1 — there is no sign-up
  • Your files — V1 does not upload your files to our servers

We may collect basic, anonymous usage data if you use a hosting provider that provides it:

  • Anonymous page views (e.g. how many people visited esktop.com) — via GitHub Pages / Vercel analytics, if enabled
  • Browser type and device type, to fix bugs and improve mobile layout
  • Error logs from our hosting provider to keep the site online

3. Cookies & LocalStorage

Esktop V1 uses LocalStorage in your browser to save your demo note. That's it. It stays on your device. You can clear it any time by clearing your browser site data for esktop.com.

We do not use advertising cookies, fingerprinting, or cross-site tracking in V1. If we add analytics in the future, we will update this policy and use privacy-friendly analytics.

4. Third-Party Links — App Store

Our App Store links to official publisher websites (for example, Microsoft, Google, VideoLAN, Mozilla). When you click Get, you are taken to that publisher's site. We do not control their privacy practices, cookies, or downloads. Please review their privacy policy before downloading.

We do not get paid by publishers for these links in V1, and we do not sell your data to them.

5. Children & Students

Esktop is designed to be safe for students. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Because V1 has no accounts and no data collection of notes or files, there is nothing for a child to submit to us. If we add accounts in the future, we will add age-appropriate protections and parental controls as required by law.

6. Data Security

We use HTTPS to protect traffic to esktop.com. Because V1 stores your demo notes only in your browser, there is no central database of your notes to leak. For future Pro features (cloud sync), we will use encrypted storage and will describe it here before launch.

7. Your Rights

Since your demo data lives in your browser, you can delete it yourself at any time:

  • Delete your note: Clear the note text in the Notes app, or clear site data for esktop.com in your browser settings
  • Stop tracking: There is no account to delete in V1. If you contact us later about a Pro account, we will delete it on request
  • Access / Correction: You control your local data directly in your browser

If you are in the EU/UK (GDPR) or California (CCPA), you have rights to access, correct, delete, and port your personal data. Contact us at the email below and we will respond within 30 days.

8. Changes To This Policy

We will update this page when we add accounts, cloud sync, payments, or analytics. The "Last updated" date at the top will change. For material changes, we will add a notice on the homepage.
